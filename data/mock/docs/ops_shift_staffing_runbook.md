# QA Shift & Staffing Runbook

**Owner:** Ops Lead | **Last verified:** 2026-04-06 | **Access tier:** team_restricted_ops

## Current shift structure

- Morning shift (6am–2pm ET): 3 QA specialists
- Afternoon shift (2pm–10pm ET): 3 QA specialists
- Overnight (10pm–6am ET): 1 QA specialist, escalation-only coverage

## Coverage rule

Overnight coverage is escalation-only (Tier 3 data discrepancies) — Tier 2 re-auth cases queue until morning shift. This was the accepted tradeoff when overnight volume was low.

## Backup coverage

If a shift is short-staffed, the on-call Ops Lead can pull from the opposite shift's last hour as overlap coverage.
