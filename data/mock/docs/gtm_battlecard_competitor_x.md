# Competitive Battlecard — vs. Legacy Offshore Verification Providers

**Owner:** GTM Strategy | **Last verified:** 2026-08-10 | **Access tier:** team_restricted_gtm

## Where we win

- **Speed:** offshore manual verification typically takes 24–48 hours per patient; our automated portal/voice agents return results in minutes, with human QA review layered on top rather than as the primary mechanism.
- **Accuracy at scale:** offshore providers rely on manual data entry, which doesn't scale linearly with call volume. Our QA specialists review flagged/low-confidence transactions only, not every transaction.
- **Traceability:** every verification has a logged, auditable trail (portal screenshot or call recording plus QA sign-off). Offshore providers typically only provide a final answer with no audit trail.

## Where we're still building

- Voice fallback isn't yet live for Open Dental or Curve Dental integrations — be upfront about this if a prospect is on either platform and needs voice coverage day one.
- We don't yet support claims or payment posting (only eligibility) — competitors bundling full RCM will point this out. Roadmap has claims next; don't commit a date without checking with Product.

## Objection handling

**"How is this different from just outsourcing to a BPO?"** — We're not a labor arbitrage play; the automation does the verification, humans do QA on exceptions. That ratio is what lets accuracy hold as volume grows, which a pure-labor model can't do without linearly adding headcount.
