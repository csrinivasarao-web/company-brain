# Customer Onboarding Playbook — Denticon

**Owner:** GTM Customer Success | **Last verified:** 2026-08-14 | **Access tier:** team_restricted_gtm

## Kickoff (Week 1)

- Confirm Denticon API credentials and practice group ID with the customer.
- Run a test batch of 20 patients through the portal-automation path before going live on full volume.
- Assign a dedicated QA specialist for the customer's first 30 days.

## Common Denticon-specific issues

- Denticon's payer mapping table needs to be reviewed per-practice-group at kickoff — a mismatch here is the most common cause of false "not covered" results in week one.
- Denticon accounts with more than 30 locations should be split into two onboarding batches rather than one big-bang go-live, per updated guidance from the last two enterprise rollouts.

## Go-live checklist

1. Payer mapping table reviewed and confirmed accurate
2. Test batch reviewed and signed off by QA
3. Customer's front-desk staff walked through the new workflow
4. Escalation contact confirmed on both sides
5. First full week monitored daily by CS
