# Ideal Customer Profile — DSO Segments

**Owner:** GTM Strategy | **Last verified:** 2026-08-18 | **Access tier:** public_internal

## Segment definitions

- **Enterprise DSO** — 50+ locations, multi-state, typically running CareStack or Denticon as their PMS. Highest ACV, longest sales cycle (4–6 months), requires security review and a signed BAA before any pilot.
- **Mid-market DSO** — 10–49 locations, usually single-state or regional. PMS is a mix of Denticon and Open Dental. Sales cycle 6–10 weeks. Champion is typically a Director of RCM (Revenue Cycle Management).
- **Small group practice** — 2–9 locations. Often still on Open Dental or a legacy PMS. Price-sensitive, fastest sales cycle (2–4 weeks), self-serve trial is the main motion.

## PMS platform fit notes

CareStack and Denticon integrations are the most mature — full portal + voice automation coverage. Open Dental has portal automation only (voice fallback not yet built). Curve Dental integration is early-stage; see Product roadmap for status before quoting a timeline to a prospect.
