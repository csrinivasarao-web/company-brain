# Pricing & Packaging — FY26

**Owner:** GTM Strategy | **Last verified:** 2026-08-12 | **Access tier:** team_restricted_gtm

## Tiers

| Tier | Target segment | Price basis | Included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | Small group practice | Per-location, monthly | Eligibility verification (portal only) |
| Growth | Mid-market DSO | Per-location, monthly, volume discount at 20+ locations | Eligibility (portal + voice), payer-coverage matrix access |
| Enterprise | Enterprise DSO | Custom, annual contract | Full ARC suite, dedicated QA reviewer, SLA guarantee |

## Discounting rules

Reps can discount up to 10% without approval. 10–20% requires GTM lead sign-off. Anything above 20%, or any multi-year term, requires VP approval — do not verbally commit to a number above 20% off in a live call.

## Notes

Enterprise tier pricing assumes the customer is on CareStack or Denticon. Open Dental or Curve Dental customers on Enterprise-tier volume should be flagged to Product before quoting, since voice automation coverage differs by PMS.
